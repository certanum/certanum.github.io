CERTANUM

Verification infrastructure for AI systems that produce consequential numbers.

A number an AI computed and a number it invented are identical on the page. Output review cannot separate them, and a tool-call record shows that a tool ran — not where its inputs came from.

Certanum is being built to bind every operand to its source, through the computation, into a signed record any third party can re-execute. An operand that cannot be resolved does not produce a wrong answer. It produces no answer.

Model-agnostic and industry-agnostic. The failure is not a property of any model or any sector. It appears wherever a statistical component supplies an operand to a deterministic calculation whose result is acted upon, recorded, or defended — and wherever a regulator already requires that figure to be evidenced, traceable, validated or reproducible.
