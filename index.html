CERTANUM

Verification infrastructure for AI systems that produce consequential numbers.

A number an AI computed and a number it invented are identical on the page. Output review cannot separate them, and a tool-call record shows that a tool ran — not where its inputs came from.

Certanum binds every operand to its source, through the computation, into a signed record any third party can re-execute. An operand that cannot be resolved does not produce a wrong answer. It produces no answer.
