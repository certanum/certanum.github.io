Certanum  /  Evidence

Evidence before assurance.

Certanum asks institutions to rely on evidence rather than assurance. The same applies to Certanum: first, what Certanum itself has built, stated as of a date; then the open standard it is designed to satisfy, published and independently inspectable — including what has not yet been established.

01Certanum’s own record

Certanum reference engine — M1.1

Designed to satisfy AP-1. Stated as of 4 October 2026.

Requirement coverage
Every normative requirement in the engine’s own specifications — record format, expression grammar, surface scanner and canonicalisation — is traced to its implementation and its tests.
Adversarial verification
Checks were deliberately disabled, one at a time, to confirm that a test detects each one’s absence.
Cross-domain execution
The same engine runs a pharmaceutical derived endpoint and an ophthalmology growth rate. The second was added without changing the engine.
Failure integrity
A missing source is refused, and compensating errors that leave the answer looking right are rejected — never replaced by a plausible number.
Architecture
Model-agnostic. Tested live so far with an OpenAI frontier model.

Detailed implementation evidence is available to pilot partners under NDA, and to investors in a technical session on request: investors@certanum.com.
